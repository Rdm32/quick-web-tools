About Web Tool Grove

Web Tool Grove is a collection of free online tools for everyday tasks. We build small, focused tools that open instantly, do one job well, and need no sign-up.

What you can do here

  • Text tools: word counter, case converter, duplicate line remover, Lorem Ipsum generator.
  • Image tools: image compressor and converters between JPG, PNG and WebP.
  • Calculators: percentage, age, date, loan and BMI calculators, plus a unit converter and countdown timer.
  • Developer and security tools: JSON formatter, Base64 encoder and decoder, password generator, QR code generator.

How we build our tools

  • Private by design. Most tools run entirely in your browser. Text you type and images you select are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Free and simple. No account, no installation, no hidden steps.
  • Works on any device. Our pages are designed to work on phones, tablets and computers, with a dark mode for comfortable reading.

Please note

Our calculators, such as the loan and BMI calculators, give estimates for general information only. They are not financial, medical or legal advice. See our Disclaimer for details.
