How the invoice generator works

This free invoice generator builds a professional invoice right in your browser. Fill in your business details, your client's details, and your line items, and the live preview at the bottom of the page updates instantly. When it looks right, use the Print / Save as PDF button and choose "Save as PDF" in the print dialog to download it. Your invoice is never uploaded to a server — everything is typed and stored only on your own device.

How to use it

  1. Enter your business name, email, phone, and address, then your client's name and address.
  2. Check the invoice number and dates. The invoice number is filled in for you, but you can change it to match your own numbering.
  3. Add line items: a description, a quantity, and a rate for each. The amount for every row is calculated automatically, and you can add or remove rows at any time.
  4. Set a tax rate or a discount if you need one. The totals update live in the preview.
  5. Click "Print / Save as PDF" and choose "Save as PDF" in the print dialog to download the invoice, or print it on paper.

What you can use it for

Freelance work. Designers, developers, writers, and consultants can send a clean invoice for each project without signing up for accounting software.

Small businesses. If you sell products or services, you can make an invoice for every order in under a minute and keep your records consistent.

One-off jobs. For a single repair, a photoshoot, or a tutoring session, a simple invoice looks far more professional than a plain email asking for payment.

Frequently asked questions

Is my invoice saved or uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything you type is processed in your browser and saved only in your browser's local storage on your own device, so the invoice is still there if you reload the page. Nothing is sent to a server or shared with anyone.

How do I download the invoice as a PDF?

Click the "Print / Save as PDF" button. In the print dialog, choose "Save as PDF" as the destination instead of a printer. The printed layout shows only the invoice itself — the form, buttons, and page header are hidden automatically.

How is the total calculated?

Each line amount is quantity multiplied by rate, and the subtotal is the sum of all lines. If you set a discount, it is taken off the subtotal first. Tax is then calculated on the amount after the discount. The total is the discounted subtotal plus the tax. For example, a subtotal of $850 with a 10% discount and 8% tax gives a discount of $85, tax of $61.20, and a total of $826.20.

Can I use a different currency?

Yes. Choose a currency from the list — US Dollar, Euro, British Pound, Japanese Yen, Chinese Yuan, and more. All amounts in the form and in the preview are formatted in that currency using your browser's standard number formatting.

Does the invoice number have to be unique?

Yes, give each invoice its own number so you and your client can refer to it later. The tool fills in a number based on today's date when you start, but you can edit it to match your own system, for example INV-2026-001.
