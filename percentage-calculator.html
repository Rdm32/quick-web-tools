How the percentage calculator works

A percentage is a number expressed as a fraction of 100. This calculator covers the four questions people ask most often and shows the result as you type.

Percent of a number

To find X% of Y, multiply Y by X and divide by 100. For example, 20% of 150 is 150 x 20 / 100 = 30.

What percent one number is of another

Divide the first number by the second and multiply by 100. For example, 30 out of 150 is 30 / 150 x 100 = 20%.

Percentage change

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, then multiply by 100. Going from 50 to 75 gives (75 - 50) / 50 x 100 = 50% increase.

Increase or decrease by a percentage

To add a percentage, multiply by (1 + percent / 100). To subtract, multiply by (1 - percent / 100). For example, 200 plus 15% is 230, and 200 minus 15% is 170.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a discount?

Use box 4. Enter the original price and the discount percentage, then read the "minus" result as the sale price.

Why is percentage change undefined when the starting value is 0?

The formula divides by the starting value, and dividing by zero has no answer. Use a different starting value or describe the change in absolute terms.

Is a 50% increase followed by a 50% decrease back to the start?

No. 100 up 50% is 150, and 150 down 50% is 75. The second percentage is applied to a larger number, so the result is lower than the start.
