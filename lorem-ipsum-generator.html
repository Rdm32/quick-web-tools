Lorem Ipsum Generator

Generate placeholder text for your designs, mockups and layouts.

How the Lorem Ipsum generator works

The Lorem Ipsum generator creates placeholder text for designs, mockups, and layouts. Choose how much text you need, from 1 to 2000, and whether you want paragraphs, sentences, or words. Click Generate and the text appears in the box, ready to copy. The text is assembled from a pool of Latin-style words, so every generation is different. A checkbox lets the first paragraph begin with the classic opening line, "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet"; it is on by default and you can untick it for a fully random passage. You can generate again for a fresh passage, copy the result, or clear the box.

How to use it

  1. Choose the type: Paragraphs, Sentences, or Words.
  2. Enter the amount you need, from 1 to 2000.
  3. Click Generate to create the placeholder text.
  4. Click Copy Text to copy it, or Generate again for a different passage.

What you can use it for

Website mockups. When you design a page before the real content is written, placeholder text shows how headings, paragraphs, and spacing will look without waiting for the copy.

Print and layout design. Brochures, posters, and magazines need realistic blocks of text to judge fonts and column widths. Lorem Ipsum fills the space without distracting anyone with readable words.

Testing fonts and themes. If you want to compare typefaces or test a new blog theme, generating a few paragraphs gives you a realistic sample to look at right away.

Frequently asked questions

What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is scrambled Latin text that has been used as placeholder copy since the 1500s. It looks like real writing but carries no meaning, which is exactly the point: readers focus on the design instead of the words.

How much text can I generate?

You can generate up to 50 paragraphs, 200 sentences, or 2000 words in one click. For a full page mockup, a few paragraphs are usually enough; for a long article layout, generate more.

Can I use Lorem Ipsum in a commercial design?

Yes. Lorem Ipsum is public-domain placeholder text with no copyright restriction, so you can use it in client work, templates, and products you sell.

Why use placeholder text instead of real words?

Real words pull attention toward what the text says, while a design review should focus on how the page looks. Placeholder text lets clients and designers judge spacing, fonts, and layout without getting distracted by unfinished copy.

Is the generated text the same every time?

No. The text is assembled from a pool of Latin-style words, so each generation produces a different passage. The first paragraph starts with the classic line, "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet", as long as the checkbox for it is ticked; untick the box if you want every paragraph to be fully random.
