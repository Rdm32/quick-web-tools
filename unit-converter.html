How the unit converter works

The unit converter changes a measurement from one unit to another. It covers four categories: length, weight, temperature, and area. Pick a category, type a value, and choose the units to convert from and to. The result appears instantly and is rounded to at most 10 decimal places. The swap button reverses the conversion, and the Copy Result button copies the answer to your clipboard.

How to use it

  1. Choose a category: Length, Weight, Temperature, or Area.
  2. Type the value you want to convert.
  3. Select the unit to convert from and the unit to convert to.
  4. Read the result, use the swap button to reverse it, or copy it with Copy Result.

What you can use it for

Travel and everyday life. Convert miles to kilometers for a road trip, pounds to kilograms at the gym, or Fahrenheit to Celsius when checking the weather abroad.

Cooking and recipes. Recipes from other countries often use grams and milliliters while yours use ounces and cups. Converting weights keeps the recipe accurate instead of guessing.

Property and land. Square feet, square meters, acres, and hectares all describe area, and listings in different countries use different ones. This tool makes it easy to compare sizes on equal terms.

Frequently asked questions

Which units are supported?

Length: meters, kilometers, centimeters, millimeters, miles, yards, feet, and inches. Weight: kilograms, grams, milligrams, metric tons, pounds, and ounces. Temperature: Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin. Area: square meters, square kilometers, square feet, square yards, acres, and hectares.

How do I reverse a conversion?

Click the swap button between the two unit boxes. It exchanges the "from" and "to" units and recalculates the result immediately.

Are temperature conversions exact?

Yes. Temperature uses the exact conversion formulas between Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin, so a result such as 32 Fahrenheit is exactly 0 Celsius.

How many decimal places does the result have?

Results are rounded to a maximum of 10 decimal places, with trailing zeros removed, so you get a precise answer that is still easy to read.

Can I convert between categories, for example length to weight?

No. A conversion only makes sense inside one category, because meters and kilograms measure different things. Pick the category that matches your measurement first.
