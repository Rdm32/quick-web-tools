How the tip calculator works

This free tip calculator works out the tip on a restaurant bill, adds it to the bill to give the total, and splits the total evenly between the people sharing it. Enter the bill amount, choose a tip percentage from the preset buttons or type a custom percentage, and set the number of people. The tip amount, total bill, amount per person, and tip per person update live as you change any value. Everything runs in your browser, so your numbers are never uploaded to a server or saved anywhere.

How to use it

  1. Enter the bill amount, before the tip. Use the amount on the check, before tax if you prefer to tip on the pre-tax amount.
  2. Tap a preset tip percentage (10%, 15%, 18%, 20%, or 25%) or type your own percentage in the custom tip box.
  3. Enter the number of people sharing the bill, from 1 to 100. Leave it at 1 if you are paying alone.
  4. Read the tip amount and total bill, then the amount per person and tip per person if you are splitting the bill.

What you can use it for

Dinner with friends. Split a restaurant check evenly in seconds instead of doing the division on a phone calculator, and see at a glance what each person owes including the tip.

Choosing a tip. Compare 15%, 18%, and 20% on the same bill to see how much each level costs before you decide how generous to be.

Group meals and outings. With up to 100 people, the calculator also works for large group dinners, office lunches, and shared celebrations where one person pays and the rest pay them back.

Frequently asked questions

How is the tip calculated?

The tip is the bill amount multiplied by the tip percentage divided by 100. The total bill is the bill amount plus the tip. For example, a $100 bill with a 20% tip gives a $20.00 tip and a $120.00 total. With 4 people sharing, that is $30.00 per person and $5.00 of tip per person.

How is the bill split between people?

The per-person split divides the total evenly: the amount per person is the total bill divided by the number of people, and the tip per person is the tip amount divided by the number of people. Everyone is assumed to pay the same share, whatever they ordered.

Why can the per-person amounts be a cent off when added up?

All money amounts are rounded to 2 decimal places, so a split that does not divide evenly can leave a cent difference. For example, a $47.50 bill with a 15% tip totals $54.63, which is $18.21 per person for 3 people, and $18.21 x 3 = $54.63 exactly in this case, but other bills can be a cent higher or lower than the total when the rounded shares are added together. In practice, one person pays the extra cent or it is settled with the change.

Should I tip on the bill before or after tax?

That is a matter of custom, and it varies by place. Many people tip on the pre-tax amount, which is the food and drink total before sales tax is added, while others tip on the full amount shown on the check. This calculator tips on whatever bill amount you enter, so enter the pre-tax amount if you want to tip on that, or the full check total if you prefer that.

Is this financial advice?

No. The results are estimates for working out a tip and splitting a bill, not financial advice. Tip customs vary by country and by service, and a service charge may already be included in the bill, so check the check before adding a tip.
