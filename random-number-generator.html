Random Number Generator

Generate random numbers in any range, for draws, games, and sampling.

How the random number generator works

This free random number generator picks numbers in any range you choose. Enter a minimum and a maximum, choose how many numbers you want, and press Generate. The numbers come from your browser's cryptographic random source, crypto.getRandomValues, rather than the weaker Math.random. If you tick "No repeats", each number appears at most once. Everything runs in your browser, so nothing is uploaded or saved.

How to use it

  1. Enter a minimum and a maximum. Both must be whole numbers, and the minimum must be smaller than the maximum.
  2. Enter how many numbers you want, from 1 to 1000.
  3. Tick "No repeats" if each number should appear at most once. The count must then fit inside the range.
  4. Press Generate, then use Copy results to copy the numbers to your clipboard.

What you can use it for

Draws and raffles. Pick a winning number fairly from a set of entries, for example a number from 1 to 100 for a prize draw.

Games and dice. Roll a virtual die, pick a random card number, or choose a starting player without a physical die or deck.

Sampling and testing. Pick random sample numbers from a list or generate test values in a chosen range for a spreadsheet or experiment.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the numbers come from?

They come from the browser's cryptographic random source, crypto.getRandomValues, which draws on randomness provided by your device and operating system. It is stronger than the Math.random function used by many simple generators.

What does "No repeats" require?

With "No repeats" checked, each number can appear at most once, so the count must fit inside the range. The range holds max minus min plus 1 different numbers, so if you ask for more numbers than that, the tool shows an error instead of generating.

Can this predict lottery numbers?

No. The results are random, and every number in the range is equally likely on each draw. A random pick cannot predict a lottery or improve your odds of winning one.

What values can I enter?

The minimum and maximum must both be whole numbers, and the minimum must be smaller than the maximum. The count must be a whole number from 1 to 1000. Decimals and empty fields are rejected with an error message.

Are my numbers saved or shared?

No. The numbers are generated in your browser on your own device and shown only on this page. They are never uploaded, stored, or shared with anyone.
