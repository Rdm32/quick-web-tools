How the countdown timer works

The countdown timer counts down live to any date and time you choose. Give the event a name, pick the target date and time, and press Start Countdown. The page shows the remaining days, hours, minutes, and seconds, updating every second. Quick buttons add one hour, one day, one week, or 30 days to the target, and the Copy Countdown button copies the current countdown as text. Your countdown is saved in the browser, so if you close the page and come back, it resumes from the saved target. When the time arrives, the page shows a celebration message that the countdown is complete.

How to use it

  1. Type an event name, for example "New Year" or "Final exam".
  2. Pick the target date and time, or use a quick button such as +1 Day or +1 Week.
  3. Click Start Countdown and watch the days, hours, minutes, and seconds tick down.
  4. Use Copy Countdown to share the countdown, or Reset to clear it and start a new one.

What you can use it for

Personal milestones. Count down to a birthday, a holiday, a wedding, or the end of a school term, and check the remaining time whenever you like.

Deadlines and launches. Keep a visible countdown to a project deadline, an exam date, or a product launch so the whole team can see how much time is left.

Daily motivation. A countdown to a goal such as a race day or a vacation makes the wait feel shorter and keeps the goal in sight.

Frequently asked questions

Will the countdown keep working if I close the page?

The target date is saved in your browser, so when you reopen the page the countdown resumes from the same target. It does not tick while the page is closed, but it recalculates the remaining time as soon as you open it again.

What happens when the countdown reaches zero?

The page shows a celebration message saying the countdown is complete, and the timer stops at zero.

Can I count down to a date in the past?

No. The target must be in the future. Pick a date and time ahead of now for the countdown to start.

Does the timer use my time zone?

Yes. The target date and time are read in your device's local time zone, so the countdown matches the clock on your own computer or phone.

Can I share the countdown with someone?

Yes. The Copy Countdown button copies the event name and the remaining time as text, which you can paste into a message or an email.
