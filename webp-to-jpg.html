Why save WebP images as JPG?

Images saved from websites often arrive as WebP, which some email apps, printers, presentation software and older devices cannot open. JPG works almost everywhere, so converting makes your pictures easy to share and use.

Frequently asked questions

What happens to a transparent WebP image?

JPG does not support transparency, so any transparent area becomes white. Use our WebP to PNG converter if you need to keep it.

Which quality setting is best?

A setting around 90 to 95 keeps images looking sharp while still giving a reasonable file size. Use a lower value when file size matters more.

Can I convert animated WebP files?

The converter saves a single still image, so animated WebP files will become one frame, not a moving picture.

Do my files leave my computer?

No. Everything happens inside your browser.
