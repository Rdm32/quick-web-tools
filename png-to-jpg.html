When should you use JPG instead of PNG?

JPG is usually much smaller than PNG for photos and screenshots with many colors. That makes it a good choice for email attachments, upload forms with size limits and web pages that need to load fast.

Frequently asked questions

What happens to transparent areas?

JPG cannot store transparency, so transparent areas are filled with white. If you need to keep transparency, stay with PNG.

What quality setting should I choose?

Around 90 gives a good balance for most images. Lower values make smaller files but may show visible blur around edges and text.

Can I convert PNG to JPG without losing quality?

JPG is a lossy format, so some detail is always discarded. At high quality settings the difference is very hard to see.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser.
