How the JPG to WebP converter works

This free converter changes JPG and PNG images into WebP files, using the canvas element in your browser. WebP keeps the image looking the same while storing it with less data, so the converted files are usually much smaller than the originals. Your files are never uploaded to a server, and your original files are never changed.

How to use it

  1. Drag your JPG or PNG files into the box above, or click it to choose files. You can add several files at once.
  2. Move the quality slider. The default is 80, a good balance between file size and image quality. A lower number makes smaller files, and a higher number keeps more detail.
  3. Each file converts right away. Check the original size, the WebP size, and the savings for each file.
  4. Press Download next to a file to save its WebP version.

What you can use it for

Websites and blogs. Smaller images load faster and use less bandwidth, which helps both visitors and search rankings, without a visible change in quality at the default setting.

Sharing and storage. Shrink a folder of photos before sending them or backing them up, so they take up less space and upload faster.

Online shops and portfolios. Product photos and portfolio images load quickly as WebP, which keeps shoppers and clients from waiting on large image files.

Frequently asked questions

How much smaller is a WebP file?

WebP files are typically 25-35% smaller than JPG files at similar quality. The exact saving depends on the image and the quality setting you choose: detailed photos save less, and simple graphics often save more.

Does the converter upload my images?

No. The conversion happens in your browser with the canvas element. Your images are never sent to a server, stored, or shared with anyone.

Will WebP files open everywhere?

All modern browsers support WebP, including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. Very old software may not open WebP files, so keep your original JPG or PNG if you need to use the image in an old program.

Does converting to WebP reduce quality?

WebP conversion at the quality setting you choose is lossy, so a small amount of detail is discarded to save space. At the default quality of 80 the change is hard to notice. A higher quality keeps more detail but makes a larger file, and converting cannot add back detail that was already lost in the original JPG.

Can I convert PNG files too?

Yes. Both JPG and PNG files are accepted. PNG transparency is preserved in the WebP output, and files that are not JPG or PNG are rejected with a message.
