This calculator gives an estimate for a fixed-rate loan with equal monthly payments. It does not include taxes, insurance, fees or changes in rate. It is for general information only and is not financial advice. Check the exact figures with your lender.

How the loan calculator works

For a fixed-rate loan, the monthly payment is calculated with the standard amortization formula: payment = P x r / (1 - (1 + r)^-n), where P is the amount borrowed, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate divided by 12), and n is the number of monthly payments.

Each payment has two parts. Early in the loan most of the payment is interest. Over time, the interest part shrinks and more of each payment goes toward the principal. The amortization schedule shows this month by month.

Frequently asked questions

How can I lower the total interest I pay?

A shorter term or a lower interest rate reduces total interest. Try changing the term in the calculator to see the difference between, for example, 15 and 30 years.

Does this work for car loans and personal loans?

Yes. Any loan with a fixed rate and equal monthly payments follows the same formula. Enter the term in months if it is shorter than a year or two.

What if the interest rate is 0%?

The loan is simply divided into equal parts, so the monthly payment equals the amount divided by the number of months.

Is my data saved or sent anywhere?

No. All calculations run in your browser and nothing is uploaded.
