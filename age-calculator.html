How the age calculator works

The calculator counts full years first, then the remaining full months, then the leftover days. A birthday counts as reached on the same day of the month. People born on the 29th, 30th or 31st are handled by using the last day of shorter months.

Total days are the real number of calendar days between the two dates. Total hours are total days multiplied by 24, so they ignore the time of day you were born.

Frequently asked questions

Can I find my age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the "Age at" date to any date after the date of birth. This is useful for school cut-off dates, legal ages and planning.

How is a leap-day birthday (February 29) handled?

In years that have no February 29, the birthday is counted on February 28.

Is my date of birth stored anywhere?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser, and nothing is uploaded or saved.
